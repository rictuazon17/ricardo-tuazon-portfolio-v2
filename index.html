Methodology

How I Troubleshoot

A systematic, structured approach to diagnosing, resolving, documenting, and preventing technical incidents.

01

Identify

Understand the symptoms and business impact. Gather user reports, error messages, and scope of affected systems.

02

Isolate

Determine whether the issue is endpoint, network, identity, application, server or cloud related.

03

Investigate

Logs, monitoring, event viewer, PowerShell, DNS tools, network diagnostics and system telemetry.

04

Resolve

Implement the safest effective fix with minimal disruption to users and business operations.

05

Validate

Confirm functionality with the user/system and verify no secondary issues were introduced.

06

Document

Record the resolution and preventive recommendations in the knowledge base or ticketing system.

07

Prevent

Perform root-cause analysis and eliminate recurring incidents through permanent fixes.
